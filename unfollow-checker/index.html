Follow-back Checker

Find who you follow that doesn't follow you back. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded and no login is needed.

1. Get your data from Instagram

  1. Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose Followers and following, format JSON, date range All time.
  3. Unzip the download. Find followers_1.json (there may be followers_2.json, ...) and following.json under connections/followers_and_following/.

2. Load the files

Keep list (never flagged)

Usernames to exclude — friends, brands, creators you follow on purpose. One per line or comma-separated. Saved in this browser.

Instagram limits how fast you can unfollow. Going a few at a time, by hand, is the safest way to avoid an action block. "Open next profile" opens the next one in a new tab and ticks it off.
